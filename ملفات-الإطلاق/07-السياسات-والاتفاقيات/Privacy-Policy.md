## Scope

This policy explains how Khadamati processes data for customer, provider,
company, and administration accounts across requests, matching, maps, offers,
chat, notifications, quality workflows, and provider subscriptions.

Khadamati is a technical matching and request-management platform. It is not a
government authority or legal certification body.

## Data processed

- Account data: name, phone number, image, governorate, wilayah, hashed access
  credential, language, and notification settings.
- Request data: category, exact service, description, priority, selected
  location, images, status, date, and time.
- Interaction data: offers, recommendations, ratings, complaints, consents,
  chats, voice messages, and locations shared within a request.
- Provider/company data: account type, services, coverage, availability,
  indicative pricing, images, licence or registration, verification documents,
  subscriptions, branches, and team data where enabled.
- Security and technical data: session identifiers, sign-in attempts, request
  timestamps, server network information when available, request IDs, and push
  subscriptions.
- Provider financial administration data: plan, amount, currency, status,
  reference, and invoice. The current system does not store card data.

## Purposes

Data is used to create and secure accounts, match requests, operate offers and
chat, show distance with permission, review providers, manage subscriptions and
complaints, prevent abuse, investigate incidents, and provide support.

## Consent and permissions

Acceptance is stored against a versioned policy. Device permission is requested
separately for location, microphone, notifications, and image access. Contact
consent is scoped to one request, one provider, and one channel and may be
revoked.

The final lawful basis for each purpose must be confirmed by Omani legal
counsel before production.

## Location and contact privacy

Precise location is used only when the person selects current location or
shares a location in a request. Manual area selection remains available. A
customer phone number stays hidden until that customer grants request-specific
consent. A paid provider plan never grants access to customer contact data.

## Provider documents

Identity, licence, registration, and verification documents are confidential
and restricted to authorized administrators for review, compliance, and
incident handling.

## Sharing and international transfers

Necessary data may be shared with matching providers, approved infrastructure
operators, OpenStreetMap tile services, and optional WhatsApp, push, OTP, or
payment providers when enabled. Production regions, contracts, and
cross-border safeguards must be confirmed before launch.

## Retention and rights

Proposed retention rules are listed in the separate retention policy. Subject
to applicable law, people may request access, correction, a copy, consent
withdrawal, restriction, portability, deletion, or complaint handling.

Account deletion can be initiated in account settings after verification. A
public web deletion path and final fulfilment periods remain required before
store submission.

## Children

The service is not directed to persons under 18 for independent accounts or
service agreements.

## Security and incidents

Controls include credential hashing, server-side sessions and authorization,
signed private media links, rate controls, and audit records. No digital
service can guarantee absolute security. Material incidents follow an approved
containment, assessment, and notification process.
