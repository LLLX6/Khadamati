# Account and Data Deletion Policy

> **This document is a draft and requires legal review and formal approval before publication.**

## Request paths

- In app: Account Settings → Delete Account → verify access code.
- Public web: `[Required before Google Play submission]`.
- Support when access is unavailable: `om.khadamati@gmail.com`.

## Verification

An account is not deleted based on a phone number alone. An authenticated
session or approved recovery process is required, with additional evidence when
fraud is suspected. Stored access codes are never sent in plaintext.

## Effect

Deletion revokes sessions, prevents sign-in and public listing, disables
provider request receipt, removes or de-identifies public profile data, and
withdraws unnecessary contact consents. A limited audit record may be retained.

Records required for a dispute, fraud prevention, accounting, or a lawful
authority request may be retained with restricted access. Final periods require
Omani legal review.

Backups may retain protected copies until their scheduled expiry. A disaster
restore process must re-apply deletion requests before restored data becomes
available.
